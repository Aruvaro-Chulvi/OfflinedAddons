# ✈️ Arcade Flow Sky Defender

*[Leer en español](README.es.md)* · **Official Offlined addon** · MIT

An arcade air-combat survival game: a giant communication tower and, wave after wave, enemy aircraft coming for it.
Shoot them down, repair the tower and hold on as long as you can. Play in the **Neon Arena**, the game's own
synthwave city that needs no downloads, or in **your city**, over a map you have downloaded. Fully offline, built on
Offlined's **Wiki Flight** engine.

## How to play

1. **⚡ Quick play** repeats your last game with one click. In **Prepare the game** you choose the battlefield (Neon
   Arena and its style, or your city: the map and what you defend, the tower at a point, at random or one of your
   last places, or one of your **3D landmarks**), the weather, your plane, the mode and the difficulty.
2. Defend the tower. Its health goes from 100 % to 0 %, and you can see the damage on the tower itself.
   - **Repair kits** fall by parachute from some enemies: fly over one, then through the green ring next to the
     tower (+15 %). Between waves the tower also recovers 5 %.
   - **Power-ups** glow in the air after some kills: rapid fire, unlimited weapon 2, ship repaired, double points.
   - **Four anti-aircraft turrets** on the tower's plaza help you while the tower is above 30 %.
   - Shooting down an enemy that had almost reached the tower is a **Saved!** (+100); any hit on the tower breaks
     your streak.
   - **Streak:** every kill raises the score multiplier up to ×8, but kills must be chained: wait too long and it is
     lost. Kills while flying **low** (under 140 m above the ground) are worth ×1.5.
   - During the break between waves you can **call the next wave now** (N, or X on the controller) and score points
     for every second you save.
3. Every 5th wave brings a **boss**, a strategic bomber: only its engines really hurt it. With two engines left it
   gets furious; with one, it dives at the tower.
4. **Every 3 waves you choose an upgrade** out of three, for that game only: more damage or fire rate, a bigger load
   for weapon 2, armour, shield, better turrets, better kits, a repair crew, a magnet, more flares or longer
   power-ups. The break waits until you choose (1-2-3, the D-pad or the mouse).
5. From wave 4 enemy fighters (and the furious boss) fire **missiles at you**: a beep warns you. Drop **flares**
   (F, or B on the controller; they recharge one by one) and turn hard, and the missile almost always goes after
   them.

Enemies come from the directions shown during the break between waves (text, orange arrows and the radar), and
many waves have a theme: tower strike, fighters, drone swarm, bomber squadron, helicopters, low-level run.

**Training** (about 3 minutes) teaches flying, shooting, chaining a streak, locking targets, dropping flares,
repairing the tower, picking upgrades and calling the wave, with whatever keys or buttons you have assigned.

When the game ends you can **watch a replay of the last 30 seconds** with film cameras (behind your plane, fly-bys,
from the foot of the tower and, if it falls, the tower coming down), at ×½, ×1 or ×2.

## Neon Arena

The game's own battlefield: a synthwave city of avenues, skyscrapers with neon crowns, glowing billboards and light
traffic, ringed by wireframe mountains, under a matching sky with a striped sun. The tower, the anti-aircraft
turrets and the searchlights wear neon too, and enemies get a glowing outline. No map is needed and it runs smoothly
on modest graphics.

- **Styles:** Sunset, Midnight and Vaporwave, or a random one each game.
- **A new city every game.** The daily game uses the day's city and style, the same for everyone; training always
  uses the same one.
- **Flow gates:** low neon rings over the main avenues. Flying through one gives a 3 s boost, raises your streak and
  scores points. They reappear somewhere else shortly after.
- Buildings are never taller than half the tower, so you can see it from anywhere in the city.

## Weather and night

| Weather | |
|---|---|
| Changing | Starts at dusk and the weather changes between waves (rain, snow where it is cold, storm; bosses often bring a storm). The next wave's weather is announced in the break. |
| Clear | Always clear; starts at dusk. |
| Storm | Storm all game. |
| Dead of night | Night from the start, and the clock does not move. |

In a storm enemies miss more and their missiles turn worse, but your radar cuts out now and then. At night four
**searchlights** sweep the sky and follow the enemies that come close.

## Defending a 3D landmark

Instead of the tower you can defend one of the **3D landmarks** you have downloaded in Wiki Flight (Offlined
Landmarks 3D) that lie inside the chosen map, in its real place. It is smaller than the tower, so it is harder to
spot from afar; with damage it catches fire and darkens. The turrets and searchlights stand around it. Not in the
daily game or in the Neon Arena (it belongs to «your city»). Those games show 🏛️ in the ranking.

## Modes

| Mode | |
|---|---|
| Short | 10 waves with 2 bosses, about 4–6 minutes. If the tower holds, you win. The default mode. |
| 5-min Blitz | 5 minutes against the clock: waves with almost no rest and no upgrades. When time is up, points for whatever is left of the tower. |
| Survival | Endless waves until the tower falls. |
| Daily | The same game for the whole family today, in the Neon Arena: same waves, same city, plane of the day, Normal difficulty. Replay it as often as you like; today's ranking keeps everyone's best. |

Difficulty: easy, normal or hard. An optional **AI wingman** (not in the daily game) flies with you and goes for
whatever threatens the tower; its kills give no points, and those games are marked 🤝 in the ranking.

## The planes

| | Light attack plane | Fighter |
|---|---|---|
| Speed | Slow | Fast |
| Agility | Very high | Medium |
| Weapon 1 | Two wing machine guns | Nose rotary cannon |
| Weapon 2 | Rocket salvos (area damage) | Guided missiles: hold to lock up to 4 targets, release to fire |

Both planes are our own generic designs, and so are the enemies (a fictional faction in dark grey and amber).
Six liveries unlock as you rank up, from Cadet to Guardian. The **Hangar** shows each plane's specs, its weapons
and its liveries.

## Controls

Arcade controls for **controller** and **keyboard and mouse**, both remappable in Controls:

| | Keyboard and mouse | Controller |
|---|---|---|
| Fly | Mouse (click the screen to aim with it) or arrows / A D | Left stick |
| Weapon 1 · Weapon 2 | Space or left click · V or right click | RT · LT |
| Target (hold: camera on it) | Tab or E | Y |
| Flares | F | B |
| Call the wave now | N | X |
| Speed up · Slow down | W or Shift · S or Ctrl | RB · LB |
| Camera · Pause | C · Esc | D-pad up · Start |

Menus, pause and results also work with the controller (D-pad or left stick to move, A to choose, B to go back,
Start to resume) or the keyboard (arrows or Tab, Enter or Space, Esc; R plays again on the results screen). On
option selectors, ← → change the choice. In Settings, «Reduce effects» turns off slow motion and softens camera shake
and red flashes.

The game pauses by itself when the window loses focus, when Offlined is minimised or when the controller is
disconnected. Leaving the addon in the middle of a game asks first: an unfinished game does not count.

## Progress

Ranks (Cadet, Pilot, Lieutenant, Captain, Ace, Guardian) by the best wave reached, 33 achievements, your history
and a **family ranking** for each mode and difficulty, with every Offlined user who plays on this computer.
When the scoring rules change, the ranking only compares games played with the new rules: since version 1.4
(streak, final waves, boss and tower bonus); before that, since 1.3 (Neon Arena, Flow gates, low flying). Older
games stay in your history and achievements.

## Sound

Sound effects are synthesised in the browser. The calm music in the menu and between waves is generated too; during
the waves the combat track «Drum and bass» by bertsz (OpenGameArt, CC0) plays in a seamless loop, with a crossfade
between the two. Separate volumes for
music and effects in Settings; the 🔊 button at the top right (or the M key) silences everything, and the game is
quiet while Offlined's global mute is on.

In Settings › «Game music» you can replace all the music with your own: a song from your Music (it loops) or one of
your playlists (it plays through and starts again). «Original» brings back the game's music.

## Requirements

- **Offlined 4.0 or later** (the addon uses Wiki Flight's add-on mode, new in Offlined 4). The Neon Arena needs an
  Offlined 4 build that supports it; with an older one you play in «your city» and the game tells you.
- Only to play in **your city**: a **fully detailed map (level 14 or more) downloaded in Maps** covering the place
  of the battle and, to defend a landmark, at least one Wiki Flight 3D landmark inside the map.
- In Settings › Graphics, «Performance» (the default on integrated graphics) draws less far, with less detail and
  no building shadows in the arena.

## Where your data is

- In the addon's own storage (`Offlined Data/Addons/installed/sky-guardians/data/storage.json`): finished games,
  rankings, achievements and ranks **of each Offlined user**. Updating keeps it; uninstalling deletes it.
- In the browser engine's local storage: your settings, controls, volumes and last places.

## Offlined API

Reads `/api/users/session` (only to know which Offlined user is playing and their name), `/api/wiki-geo` (towns
near the chosen point, for «Random place»), `/api/landmarks3d/status` (which 3D landmarks you have) and its own storage `/api/addons/sky-guardians/storage`. It runs inside
Offlined's Wiki Flight (`/flightsim/`) and uses Offlined's copy of three.js (`/vendor/three-0.169.0/`). Nothing
else, and nothing from the internet.
