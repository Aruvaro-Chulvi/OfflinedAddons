# Code of conduct

*Español abajo.*

Offlined is used by families, schools and people far from good internet. We want this repository to be as
welcoming as the app.

- **Be kind and patient.** Many contributors are writing their first addon, or writing in a language that is not
  their own.
- **Criticise code, not people.** Explain why, and suggest how.
- **Assume good faith**, and ask when something is unclear.
- **No harassment**, insults, discrimination, sexual content or personal attacks, here or about people from here.
- **Respect privacy**: never publish someone else's personal data.

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close pull requests and issues, and block people who do not follow
these rules. To report a problem privately, use the contact at [offlined.org](https://www.offlined.org).

---

## Español

Sé amable y paciente; critica el código, no a las personas; supón buena fe y pregunta si algo no está claro. No
se admite acoso, insultos, discriminación, contenido sexual ni ataques personales, ni publicar datos personales
de nadie. El mantenedor puede editar o borrar comentarios, cerrar pull requests e issues y bloquear a quien no
respete estas normas. Para avisar en privado, usa el contacto de [offlined.org](https://www.offlined.org).
